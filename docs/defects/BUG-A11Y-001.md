# BUG-A11Y-001 — Form controls unnamed, one region unreachable, dialogs not announced

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Module | Accessibility (`A11Y`) |
| Severity | High |
| Priority | High |
| Status | **Closed — fixed 2026-09-26** |
| Found by | First axe-core scan, 2026-09-26 |
| Requirements | `A11Y-001`, `A11Y-002`, `A11Y-003`, `A11Y-007` |

## Summary

The first accessibility scan of the application found four distinct faults across
24 views. Three were detected by axe-core; the fourth was not detectable by it
and was found by driving the interaction.

## What was found

Scanned against WCAG 2.1 A and AA with axe-core 4.13, across every customer and
back-office view plus an open modal.

| Rule | Impact | Where | Requirement |
|---|---|---|---|
| `label` | critical | 73 form controls across the application, including sign-in email and password, the MFA code, and every field in the transfer modal | `A11Y-001` |
| `select-name` | critical | The three selects in the transfer modal, plus the account and filter selects on transactions and statements | `A11Y-003` |
| `color-contrast` | serious | `.nav-label`, the sidebar section heading, at 3.73:1 against its background where 4.5:1 is required | `A11Y-002` |
| `scrollable-region-focusable` | serious | The audit log's scrolling table container, reachable with a pointer but not with a keyboard | `A11Y-001` / 2.1.1 |

A fifth fault, invisible to the scanner: modals carried no `role="dialog"`, moved
no focus on open, trapped no focus while open, could not be closed with Escape,
and returned focus nowhere on close.

## Why every form in the application failed the same way

The markup was consistent and consistently wrong:

```html
<div class="field"><label>Amount</label><input name="amount" ...></div>
```

The label is adjacent to the control, not associated with it. Sighted users read
the pairing from the layout; nothing in the markup states it, so assistive
technology announces an unlabelled input. One pattern, repeated 73 times, is why
a single root cause produced the largest violation count in the project.

## Fix

**Naming.** Each of the 73 controls now carries `aria-label` matching its visible
label text, and the eight filter controls that had no visible label at all were
named explicitly. The eight statement-summary `<label>` elements that precede a
`<strong>` rather than a control were left alone; they are display text, not
form labels.

A note on the choice, because it is a trade rather than the ideal. A real
`<label for="…">`, or nesting the control inside its label, would additionally
give click-to-focus — clicking "Amount" would focus the field. That was not done:
`.field` is a flex column whose spacing depends on the label and control being
siblings, so nesting needs a CSS change, and `for`/`id` needs 73 unique ids
across templates that re-render. `aria-label` satisfies the requirement and the
scanner without touching layout. The click-to-focus gap is real and recorded
here rather than glossed over.

**Contrast.** `.nav-label` moved from `#59746d` to `#6f8d85` against `#081310`,
which takes it from 3.73:1 to roughly 5.2:1.

**Keyboard reach.** Every `.table-wrap` gained `tabindex="0"`, so a scrolling
region can be scrolled without a pointer. Applied to all 15, not only the audit
table that happened to overflow with the current seed data.

**Dialogs.** Modals now carry `role="dialog"`, `aria-modal="true"` and an
`aria-label` from their heading. Focus moves to the first useful control on open,
is confined while the dialog is up, Escape closes it, and focus returns to the
control that opened it.

## Verification

`playwright/tests/accessibility/accessibility.spec.ts` — 30 tests.

Scans cover 23 views plus an open modal, and assert **zero** violations rather
than a tolerated list. The modal is scanned while open, because injected markup
is never seen by a scan of the view behind it, which is exactly how an unlabelled
form inside a dialog survives a green run.

Four tests cover what the scanner cannot decide: the dialog role and name, focus
entering and being confined, Escape closing with focus returned to its trigger,
and a status message announced without taking focus. Two more assert sign-in is
completable with the keyboard alone and every sidebar destination is a real
focusable control rather than a clickable div.

Before: 5 violation types across 24 views. After: 0.

## What this does not claim

Automated checks find a minority of WCAG issues. Nothing here establishes that
the reading order makes sense, that an announcement is coherent, or that the
application is usable with a screen reader in practice. `A11Y-007` to `A11Y-009`
are marked in the catalog as only partly machine-detectable for that reason. A
green run is a floor.
